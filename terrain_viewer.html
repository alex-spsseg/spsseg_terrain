About these numbers
Click or tap a tree to read its height: the tallest point of the point cloud within 10 ft of the click. It is not one measured tree, because a taller neighbor’s branch can raise it, and thin treetops can read low. Under More: canopy cover is the share of the ground within 30 ft that has anything taller than 6.5 ft above it. Shade is the share of the sunny day that trees block the sun at that spot. Open the Shade tool to turn them on and pick the date.
